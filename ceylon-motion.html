— The Brief —

Ceylon Motion Pictures came to IONIK Web to launch the official site for their feature film — a story confronting cyberbullying and mental health in Sri Lanka. We built a bilingual, cinematic production website: synopsis, full cast and crew directory, a curated production gallery, and a dedicated awareness section linking directly to Sri Lanka's national suicide prevention hotline.
